Asset probe temporary lab, 2026-10-14

The question: do short part lists with named materials draw objects at least as well as our props, in every view? Each object below is a few lines of text (a barrel is three turned profiles of oak and iron), drawn by a small renderer at game size from any view and facing, with our five-tone shading, patterns, lit top edges, creases and outline. Beside it: the hero for scale and our closest flat prop, on a light and a dark Emberdeep floor. 24 are written by hand; 8 are converted from Kenney's free 3D kits (CC0); 6 materials are fitted from ambientCG's texture sets (CC0).

The bar holds the look decisions, set to what was decided; change them to compare. Room shows the objects drawn by the engine in a room, beside the props they would replace (1-4 pick the view).

Converted models: Kenney's Survival, Graveyard and Furniture kits (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. Fitted materials: ambientCG (ambientcg.com), CC0 1.0. Our props are the engine's own (r.prop).
