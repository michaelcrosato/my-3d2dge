What carries over from the engine

  • Comes across: the colors (its tone ramps), the floor's stone texture, the wall bricks, the converted crate, and the hero: the engine draws him from your exact angle every frame, then he's pasted in like a sprite in Doom.
  • Had to be rewritten: perspective itself; walls, floor and boxes drawn in 3D; outlines (found from depth here, not drawn around each character).
  • Missing: the engine's lighting, cut-away walls, x-ray, particles.
  • Not quite right: the hero and the flat props are flat cut-outs, so up close or from straight above they read as pictures; the far floor shimmers; there's no ceiling.
