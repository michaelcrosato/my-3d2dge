Shapes vs props temporary comparison

Our props today are flat pictures: a crate is the same image in every camera view, can only face one way (or its mirror), and from straight above you still see its front. Converted shapes are real 3D: free 3D models (two CC0 kits) turned into small text and drawn by our engine from every angle, with our pixel shading and outline. No filter and no images.

Below are twelve models, ranked from the best case to the worst. Each row shows the hero for scale, our closest flat prop (it looks the same in every view), the shape in the game's four views at game size, and the shape turning. Everything is drawn live by the engine at game size, then enlarged. Shapes here use the prototype encoding (boxes only). Where the planned encoder would change the result, the card says so.

Cards are judged on how the shape looks at game size; the cost (boxes and lines of text) is shown beside it.

Models: Kenney's Furniture Kit (kenney.nl) and KayKit's Dungeon Pack 1.0 by Kay Lousberg, both CC0 (public domain). Our flat props are the engine's own (r.prop).
