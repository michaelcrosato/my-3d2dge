Swarming monsters find their way round pillars and up the stairs, and take turns: up to three attack at once, each after a wind-up with a red warning arc. Kills stay down until the whole wave is cleared. Then the hero cheers and the wave returns.

Physics

Every monster is a Rapier body: the crowd pushes and piles up by itself, a hit's knockback is momentum, and the fallen fly and slide.
