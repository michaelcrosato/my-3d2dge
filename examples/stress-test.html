Load

Drawing time per humanoid with outlines off: sticks 1×, skeletons 2×, HD husks 3×, knights 4× (outlines narrow the gap). Slimes grow horns, ears or wings.

Swarming monsters take turns: up to three attack at once, each after a wind-up with a red warning arc. Kills stay down until the whole wave is cleared. Then the hero cheers and the wave returns.

Camera

Pulls back by rendering more pixels at the same pixel size, so more of the hall and more monsters are on screen.

Zoom redraws everything larger or smaller at the same resolution: zooming out fits more monsters on screen without adding pixels. Each new turn angle bakes the floor once.

Any view the engine can draw: tilt 0 is the side view, 90 straight down. Zoom and the turn keys still apply on top.

Fixed, the camera stays where it is while the hero moves on, in any view. The link opens this camera (view, turn, tilt, zoom, and where it is fixed).

Chase, first-person and fly cameras need real 3D: they are in the 3D engine's starter.
