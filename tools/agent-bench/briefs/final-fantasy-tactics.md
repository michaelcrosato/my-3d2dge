SOURCE: Final Fantasy Tactics, a battle
GAME ENGINE: my-3D2dge, the handoff package in this directory (start with README.md)

Make a game: an adaptation of the source on this engine, a complete vertical slice that plays from a title screen to an
end: one battle on an isometric map of heights that turns in quarters, five units a side with jobs (a knight, an
archer, a black mage, a white mage, a thief), turns by charge time, moves and jumps over heights, attacks from the side
and behind doing more, spells with areas, and a win or a loss. Put it in game/: game/index.html plays it,
game/game.js holds it, game/test.mjs tests it without a browser. Check it with node tools/check.mjs game/index.html
before you finish.
