SOURCE: Street Fighter II, a one-on-one match
GAME ENGINE: my-3D2dge, the handoff package in this directory (start with README.md)

Make a game: an adaptation of the source on this engine, a complete vertical slice that plays from a title screen to an
end: two fighters to choose from, each with light, medium and heavy punches and kicks, a special move by a motion of
the stick (a quarter circle, a charge), blocking, throws, hit-stun and combos; two rounds won of three on a timer
against a CPU, with a versus mode for two players. Put it in game/: game/index.html plays it, game/game.js holds it,
game/test.mjs tests it without a browser. Check it with node tools/check.mjs game/index.html before you finish.
