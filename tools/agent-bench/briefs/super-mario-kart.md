SOURCE: Super Mario Kart, a Grand Prix race
GAME ENGINE: my-3D2dge, the handoff package in this directory (start with README.md)

Make a game: an adaptation of the source on this engine, a complete vertical slice that plays from a title screen to an
end: a circuit of five laps against seven drivers, hops that drift and charge a boost, item boxes (a boost, a shell, a
peel), off-road that slows you, a coin count, the places and a results screen. Put it in game/: game/index.html plays
it, game/game.js holds it, game/test.mjs tests it without a browser. Check it with node tools/check.mjs
game/index.html before you finish.
