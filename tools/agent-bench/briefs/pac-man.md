SOURCE: Pac-Man, a maze and its four ghosts
GAME ENGINE: my-3D2dge, the handoff package in this directory (start with README.md)

Make a game: an adaptation of the source on this engine, a complete vertical slice that plays from a title screen to an
end: a maze of dots and power pellets, four ghosts that each choose where to go their own way and turn blue and flee
after a pellet, a fruit, lives, a level cleared and the next one faster, a high score table with initials and an
attract mode. Put it in game/: game/index.html plays it, game/game.js holds it, game/test.mjs tests it without a
browser. Check it with node tools/check.mjs game/index.html before you finish.
